## 4. Open items Gate B waits on

From the starting prompt §5. Each line ends with Claude's proposal, for Michel's word.

1. **The grantee** — Michel personally, a fiscal sponsor, or no money until the core body is formed. *Proposal: search for fiscal sponsors in Gate B so the choice is real; take no money personally beyond the planning grant.*
2. **The ceiling** — one grant of the recommended size, or several small ones; a Via Transilvanica membership scheme or not. *Proposal: several small grants, the first being N-14; membership decided after Gate 4.*
3. **The scientific partner's standing** — which part of the agreement they sign, what they may publish, S-14 as the floor. *Proposal: a written research note modelled on the funder's part — no say over what is published on the map, no access to hosts' data beyond what each host consents to, no host identifiable, findings shown to the co-creators before publication.*
4. **The hromada councils** — approached as implementers at Gate C, or only shown the corridor as S-14 proposes. *No proposal; the stress tests warn both ways.*
5. **The country** — Romanian partners (Maramureș, the grassland studies, Via Transilvanica) in scope now, or only after the corridor is walked. *Proposal: in scope for creators and scientific reporters now, as sources of method; not as partner initiatives until Gate 4.*
6. **What Michel will not take** — money or partnership that asks for a calendar, ratings, a commission, a logo on the map, an exclusive, or a host's data. *Proposal: all six, so Gate B drops such candidates on sight.*

Gate B also notes where a candidate's fit turns on a decision still open in the checklist — most often S-19 (whether a partner keeps Part I in full, §3 included) and S-7 (what Gate 4 records).
