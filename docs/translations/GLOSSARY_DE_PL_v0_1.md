# German and Polish — the glossary

*Michel's word, October 11, 2026: the overview, the proposal and the
white paper in German and Polish. Every German and Polish text uses the
words below, so that the project says the same thing in each. They are
Claude's proposals. Nobody whose language it is has read them yet; when
someone does, this list is corrected first and the texts follow.*

## How the texts are written

- **Faithful, not summarised.** Every sentence, figure, date, source
  number `[n]`, table row and decision of the English is carried. Nothing
  added, dropped, softened or sharpened.
- **The name stays English: Trans-Carpathian Pathways.** In German
  «Transkarpatien» and in Polish «Zakarpacie» name Zakarpattia oblast,
  the misreading the review found in «Транскарпатські стежки» (D-1).
- **German:** formal register; gender-fair forms without asterisks or
  colons — paired forms at a term's first mention and in tables
  (*Gastgeberinnen und Gastgeber*), neutral forms in running text where
  they read naturally (*Gastgebende*, *Mitgestaltende*); German
  quotation marks „…“; spaced en dash – for a dash; dates as
  *10. Oktober 2026*; numbers as *25.700 €*, *4,5-mal*.
- **Polish:** standard register; the generic masculine plural for people
  (*gospodarze*, *kuratorzy*), with *gospodyni* where the source names
  the hostess; Polish quotation marks „…”; spaced en dash – for a dash;
  dates as *10 października 2026*; numbers as *25 700 €*, *4,5 raza*.
- **Quotations from English sources** are translated and keep their
  quotation marks and their source number; an established translation
  is used where one is known.
- **Sources:** titles stay in their own language; only the words around
  them (dates, "secondary", "unverified", "accessed") are translated;
  URLs unchanged; numbering unchanged.
- **Ukrainian words in italics** (*прочанин плаю*, *проща*, *прощати*)
  stay as they are, with their gloss translated.
- **People and organisations keep the names they write themselves in
  Latin script:** Michel Garand, Vasyl Yuskiv, Zhyva Khata, UBEC. Polish
  declines them as it declines any foreign name (*przez Michela
  Garanda*, *przez Vasyla Yuskiva*); *Zhyva Khata* is not declined.
- **"Michel's word, October 10, 2026"** → *Michels Entscheidung,
  10. Oktober 2026* · *decyzja Michela, 10 października 2026*.
