Where the meadows were made by mowing and grazing, holding still means losing. Less harm is not enough.

After Reed (2007) and Gibbons (2020) · 07

Sustainable development, defined in 1987, asks how an activity can continue with less harm; it balances economy, society and environment, and the industry is one of the parties whose needs it balances. Bukovel has a sustainability office; the same owners planned the Svydovets mega-resort. Regeneration, after Reed and Gibbons, starts from the place: its question, its people, its own measure of health. Doing no harm remains the floor, not the aim. The project's test at every stage: is this place more alive, and more able to go on, than it was before the pathway?
