Sources: ReHERIT heritage profile of Kosiv; UNESCO; Suspilne · 04

The craft economy is alive and concentrated in Kosiv. The painted ceramics tradition was inscribed by UNESCO in December 2019. The brynza indication was registered in September 2020 and is managed by the Association of Carpathian Vatahy in Verkhovyna. Displaced-person registrations overstate presence, but some have stayed to work, for example an atelier relocated from Kakhovka that now employs fifty women from Kosiv and its villages. Many of these people were not asked when the mountains' future was planned.
