plai

In the speech of these mountains, a path that follows a ridge from one pasture to the next.

The name also recalls the Lviv society Plai of 1924, which joined the study of the land, trail marking and the first Ukrainian mountain shelter.

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 06

The project does not start from zero. Plai is the Carpathian hub of UBEC Mapservice: PostgreSQL and PostGIS, MapServer and Mapbender, about 32 million features from OpenStreetMap with hiking routes drawn on top. Pathways is a view of Plai's entries, not a separate system: one place to enter, one consent model, one Curator queue. Photos are cleaned of location and camera data before storage. The project claims no descent from the 1924 society; it takes the name in the same spirit.
