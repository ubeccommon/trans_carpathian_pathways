The idea in one line

Travel that keeps a way of life alive, rather than putting it on display.

Hosts share what they really have — a bed, a meal, guidance, a craft — from where they live. A Pilgrim of the Plai comes to learn, not to look.

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 02

This is the whole project in one sentence. Encounter rather than extraction: a pilgrim sleeps under a roof, eats at a table, learns how something is made, and leaves money and attention with the person who holds the knowledge. The host gains a reason, and a means, to keep that knowledge alive.
