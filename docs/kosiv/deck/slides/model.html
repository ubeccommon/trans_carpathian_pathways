How it works

Three things interlock

Hosts

People who share something real from where they live, and describe it as it is. Each sets their own months, capacity, languages and access.

The pathway

Not drawn from above. It emerges as hosts join, follows existing trails, and links springs, viewpoints and places worth arriving at.

Curators and stewards

People of the corridor. The Curator suggests, never sells, a journey and holds the safety knowledge; stewards care for each stretch.

A host can offer

a bed

a meal

local guidance

a workshop

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 05

Hosts are not service providers; they choose to share what they have. The pathway is the line that joins them, adjusted by the Curator for terrain, season, capacity and the human logic of a journey. The Curator is usually a local farmer, teacher or walker, and reads every place before it is published. The pathway steward, a role added in October 2026, cares for a stretch and may enter a place for a host who cannot. Travelers need no account.
