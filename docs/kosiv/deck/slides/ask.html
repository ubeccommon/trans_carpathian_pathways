Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 11

We are not asking the Department to choose hosts or to pass on anyone's name; introductions only, and each person decides whether to join. The project does not give legal advice to hosts, which is why local guidance on Procedure 297, meals and the tourist tax matters. Ukraine takes the presidency of the Carpathian Convention after its eighth Conference of the Parties at the end of 2026; Pathways can be one concrete example of the commitment to cultural landscapes and shared routes.
