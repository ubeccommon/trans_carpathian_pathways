Guards

What the project will not do

No booking calendar

Availability is a conversation with the Curator, not slots to fill.

No ratings

A score teaches a host to perform for the score.

No commission

The project takes no share of what a traveler pays a host.

No staged tradition

The encounter is real, or it is nothing.

No one speaks for a village

A host speaks for their own place; the project for itself.

No data sold

Nothing sold, lent or tracked; every host can withdraw.

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 09

Each refusal answers a known failure of community tourism elsewhere. Fairbnb, the cooperative alternative to Airbnb, kept a commission and went into liquidation; Via Dinarica was donor-built and still depends on a support centre after twelve years. A calendar is the first step to a booking service; ratings turn hosts into performers. Payment is the host's to set: a fixed or suggested contribution, an exchange of work, or a community currency.
