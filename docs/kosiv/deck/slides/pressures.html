Average house listed at about 11.7 million hryvnia in Polianytsia against 1.9 million in Kosiv, October 2025: that gap is what makes the corridor attractive to buyers. Oblast tourist-tax receipts rose from 10.4 million hryvnia in 2021 to 46.2 million in 2025. Cattle in private households fell from 109,500 in 2021 to 76,000 in 2024; the polonynas were made by mowing and grazing and degrade when that stops. At the September 2026 summit, energy was 7.28 of the 10.7 billion dollars of listed projects. None of this is an argument against energy or work in the mountains; it is an argument that the mountains' future is being decided quickly, and mostly by people who will not live in them.
