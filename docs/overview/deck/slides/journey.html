A journey

From a host's point to a pilgrim's door

1

The host places their point

On Plai, with the Curator or a steward beside them, consenting field by field.

2

The Curator reads it

Publishes it or holds it. Safety knowledge stays off the platform.

3

A pilgrim asks

Says how much time they have, who comes along, and what they hope for.

4

The Curator suggests

A route between hosts, by season and capacity. Suggested, never sold.

5

The pilgrim walks

Stays, eats, learns, and pays the host directly, in the form the host chose.

A pilgrim may also find a published host on the map and go directly. Pilgrims need no account.

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 06

Each host chooses, for each offer, how they are paid: a fixed contribution, a suggested contribution, an exchange of work, or ubec-pay or a community currency. Payment passes directly from pilgrim to host; the project, its Curators and stewards hold no money for stays and take no share. A host may place their point at a nearby meeting place rather than the house, and say so. Every host can withdraw their place at any time; withdrawal removes it from every map and export on Plai, though not from copies someone may already have made.
