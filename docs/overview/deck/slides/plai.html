An open-source map of the whole Carpathian range, run by UBEC on its own server in the European Union.

  • A host's place sits exactly where they put it
  • Consent field by field: each person decides what is shown
  • Withdrawal removes a place from every map and export

  • No advertising, no tracking, no data sold or lent

plai

In the speech of these mountains, a path that follows a ridge from one pasture to the next.

The name also recalls the Lviv society Plai of 1924, which joined the study of the land, trail marking and the first Ukrainian mountain shelter.

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 07

The project does not start from zero. Plai is the Carpathian hub of UBEC Mapservice: PostgreSQL and PostGIS, MapServer and Mapbender, about 32 million features from OpenStreetMap with hiking routes drawn on top, and since October 2026 relief and contour lines from the GEDTM30 ground model. Pathways is a view of Plai's entries, not a separate system: one place to enter, one consent model, one Curator queue. Photos are cleaned of location and camera data before storage. Every entrant ticks that they have read the co-creator agreement. Everything can be exported at any time, so no community depends on UBEC to keep its own data. The project claims no descent from the 1924 society; it takes the name in the same spirit.
