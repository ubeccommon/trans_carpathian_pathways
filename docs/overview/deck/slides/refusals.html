Guards

What the project will not do

No booking calendar

Availability is a conversation with the Curator, not slots to fill.

No ratings

A score teaches a host to perform for the score.

No commission

The project takes no share of what a pilgrim pays a host.

No staged tradition

The encounter is real, or it is nothing.

No one speaks for a community

A host speaks for their own place; the project for itself.

No data sold

Nothing sold, lent or tracked; every host can withdraw.

Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 08

Each refusal answers a known failure of community tourism elsewhere. A calendar is the first step to a booking service, which turns a host into a supplier with slots to fill. Ratings turn hosts into performers. The cooperative alternative to Airbnb, Fairbnb, kept a commission and went into liquidation. A corridor shown to a ministry or a funder is the places of named hosts, never what a community wants; only a community can say that.
