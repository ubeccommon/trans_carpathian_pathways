Trans-Carpathian Pathways · 16

The pilot asks people on the ground first and software second, because the map already exists. Funding pays for finding and visiting hosts, a paid first Curator under a written arrangement, walking the corridor, the two legal reads, translation and hosting. A funder gets an open account at each gate, but no say over what is published or which journeys are suggested, and no access to hosts' or pilgrims' data. We also seek technical contributors. Communities: not endorsement but attention, a reading, and word passed to people who already host, teach, herd or mow that the project exists, so that they decide for themselves whether to come forward. Ukraine holds the presidency of the Carpathian Convention after its eighth conference at the end of 2026. Documents are under CC BY-SA 4.0 and code under AGPL-3.0; those published for review are on the project's site, and the record of decisions is shared with those who take part.
