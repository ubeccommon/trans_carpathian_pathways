No phase is tied to a date: each opens when the one before has proved itself · 11

The Carpathian arc is one living region across the borders of seven countries or more. Pathways is bioregional before it is national. A partner initiative in each country opens its own corridor, in its own language, with its own hosts, stewards and Curators, under the same commitments on data, consent, safety and money, and with the right to leave with its data. Which countries come first is still open. On corridors, three conditions: a host agreed to be on a map, not to become evidence in a protection case, so that use needs their separate consent; a corridor crosses land of people who never joined; and a protection designation can itself raise land prices and ban the mowing that made the meadows.
