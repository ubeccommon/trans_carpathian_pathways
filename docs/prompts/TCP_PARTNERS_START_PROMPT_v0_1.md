*Paste everything below the line as the first message of a new
conversation in the Claude Project "Trans Carpathian Pathway". It
opens the search for partners: who could fund, help create, help
implement and scientifically report a prototype of
Trans-Carpathian Pathways in the corridor from Vorokhta through
Verkhovyna to Kosiv — a prototype whose purpose is to support and
facilitate the people who build it, the co-creators of the
agreement. It produces documents in this repository and reaches no
one on its own: every approach to a partner is Michel's.*

* * *

## 0. What this conversation is for

The project has its documents, its map, its agreement and its
stress tests. It has no host by name, no Curator by name but
Michel, no money and no legal body. What it needs next is other
people: those who would pay for the first corridor's pilot, those
who would help build what the pilot lacks, those on the ground who
would help it happen, and those who would study it and say, in a
form other people trust, what happened.

This conversation finds them. Its output is a partner map and the
material to approach them with — not a funding application, not a
signed partnership, not a study design, each of which is its own
later conversation on Michel's word. It explores; it does not
commit.

**The prototype it is for.** The first corridor as the proposal
v0.3 sets it out: three to five hosts found and entered with the
Curator beside them; a Curator and the first stewards; the
corridor walked end to end in two seasons; the record kept — Gates
3 and 4 of the starting prompt; eighteen months from the first
hosts; about €25,700 as recommended, not yet decided. "Support and
facilitate the co-creator actors" means the prototype's measure is
whether the people in it — hosts, stewards, Curator, travelers,
contributors — could do their part and wanted to go on, not how
many travelers came. The white paper's §12 says what success and
failure look like; the regenerative test behind both is whether
the place is more alive and more able to go on than before the
pathway.

Not in scope: the stack (mapservice), the site
(`docs/prompts/TCP_WEB_PRESENCE_START_PROMPT`), the ten decisions
still open in `docs/DECISIONS_CHECKLIST` — except where a
partner's fit turns on one, in which case the conversation says so
and leaves it to Michel.

## 2. What is known already

**What the pilot needs**, from proposal v0.3's funding table: host
outreach; a paid Curator and paid stewards under written
arrangements; the corridor walked twice; development (Plai
changes, the site, the planner only if needed); translation;
hosting and security; two legal reads (data protection in Germany,
tourism and tax law in Ukraine) and the forming of the core body;
contingency. The budget is a recommendation for Michel's word.
Nothing is budgeted for hosts' income, software for travelers or a
second corridor.

**What binds any partnership**, from the agreement: a funder gives
no say over what is published or which journeys are suggested, and
gets no access to hosts' or travelers' data; what came in and what
it was spent on is reported at each gate; a partner initiative
keeps Part I's commitments on data, consent, safety and money
(whether §3 too — no calendar, no ratings, no commission — is
open, S-19); a public list of partners is kept from the start; the
first corridor's co-creators decide Phase 2 governance; the name
and the marks are Michel's, CC BY-SA, and a core body to be formed
will hold them. Nothing on any partner's site or in any report
names, pictures or locates a host.

**What the funder's stress test said**, and a partner will say
again: fund conditionally — a planning-and-legal grant before the
pilot; a legal body or fiscal sponsor as grantee; the Curator's
pay with an exit; a stop rule; a cost-carrier after the grant.
Several of its conditions are now met or decided (payment direct,
hosts sign on paper, a body to be formed, Curators and stewards
paid roles once financing is secured); the budget page exists; the
rest is in the checklist. The conversation does not hide what is
open from a prospective partner.

**What a scientific partner would study.** The white paper's §12,
"What is not yet known": the age of the corridor's landholders and
what happens to their land; how many newcomers have settled and
why; what accession is doing to land prices; whether the
Carpathian Eight takes up heritage and nature; what the corridor's
own people call the stewardship the project describes. And the
prototype itself as a case: a baseline before the first host, the
indicators the project set itself (hosts still hosting after three
seasons, a young person learning a meadow's work, money staying in
the villages, a community using the map to speak for its land),
the regenerative test, and the honest counting the comparables
lacked — enquiries, journeys, host-nights, earnings aggregated,
houses sold on the corridor, hosts by village (S-7, to be defined
with the partner). Ethics follow the agreement: consent for every
use beyond the map (S-14), no host identifiable in any
publication, the hromada council's voice recorded where a corridor
is drawn.

**The vocabulary partners already use**, from the white paper:
IUCN Category V, UNESCO's continuing landscape, the Carpathian
Convention's cultural landscapes, the C8 declaration's
"traditional way of life of mountain communities" and "shared
cultural and tourist routes", regenerative tourism, High Nature
Value grassland, the Convention's Ukrainian presidency after COP8.
The conversation speaks to each partner in the words that partner
uses, and never lets the words replace the measure.

**Where the project stands with people**: Michel personally
answers for it; the first Curator is Michel; the core body is to
be formed; the Ukrainian texts have not yet been read by anyone in
the corridor. A partner is also a reader.

## 3. The four kinds of partner, and what each is asked

For each kind the conversation researches real candidates — with
WebSearch and WebFetch, every claim about a candidate sourced and
dated, nothing asserted from recall about who funds what — and
records for each: who they are; what they fund, build, do or
study; why the fit (in their words and in ours); what they would
ask of the project (the funder's test is the template); what the
project would ask of them; the route to them (a programme, a call,
a named office, a person Michel knows); a deadline if there is
one; and the first open item their fit turns on.

- **Funders.** Foundations and programmes that fund rural,
  heritage, nature, mountain or community work in Ukraine and the
  Carpathians, at the pilot's scale and for a project with no
  legal body yet: EU instruments reaching Ukraine (cross-border,
  cultural, Horizon's smaller lines); the Carpathian Convention's
  and the C8's own instruments if any; Ukrainian funds;
  foundations in Germany, Switzerland, Canada and the Nordic
  countries with Ukraine programmes; the membership and
  corporate-donor model Via Transilvanica runs on; and the fiscal
  sponsors that could receive a grant before the core body exists.
- **Creators.** Those who would help build what the pilot lacks:
  designers and developers for Plai and the site; translators and
  a Ukrainian editor from the corridor; the Kosiv craft
  institutions and the makers who could shape what a workshop host
  offers; mappers who know the trail network and the `Карпатські
  стежки` foundation's marking.
- **Implementers.** Those on the ground: the three hromadas
  (Vorokhta, Verkhovyna, Kosiv) and their councils; the national
  parks the corridor touches (Carpathian, Hutsulshchyna,
  Verkhovynskyi); the Association of Carpathian Vatahy and the
  polonyna cheese producers; craft centres and museums in Kosiv
  and Yavoriv; mountain rescue and the border detachment, for the
  stage register; local NGOs and initiatives already keeping the
  heritage; the people whose voice was not asked.
- **Scientific reporters.** A university or institute that would
  document the prototype as a case study and publish it: in
  Ukraine (Ivano-Frankivsk, Lviv, Chernivtsi, Kyiv); abroad, where
  the Carpathian grassland, mountain-tourism and amenity-migration
  work the white paper cites is done; the Science for the
  Carpathians network; the Convention's own working groups. The
  ask is a study design with the project, not of it: baseline,
  indicators, counting, ethics, and a report in both languages the
  corridor can read.

## 4. Gates

Gate by gate; a gate opens on Michel's word. Claude proposes,
Michel decides, and no candidate is approached by anyone but
Michel.

- **Gate A — what the prototype needs, and from whom.** A one-page
  needs list drawn from §2: each need, its cost line in the
  proposal, and which kind of partner meets it. Michel adds what
  he already has — people he knows, approaches already made, doors
  already closed. Output: `docs/partners/NEEDS_v0_1.md`.
- **Gate B — the long list.** Twenty to forty candidates across
  the four kinds, researched and sourced, in one table with the
  fields in §3; Ukrainian-language search for the implementers and
  the Ukrainian funders. Flag every candidate whose fit turns on
  an open decision. Output: `docs/partners/LONG_LIST_v0_1.md`.
  Michel strikes and adds.
- **Gate C — the short list.** Eight to twelve candidates, each
  with a page: the fit argued both ways, what they would require,
  what the project would have to decide or build first, the route
  and the timing, and the risks — a funder who wants a logo on a
  hosts' map, a partner who wants a booking layer, a study that
  would name a host. Output: `docs/partners/SHORT_LIST_v0_1.md`,
  and the open decisions each one needs listed against the
  checklist.
- **Gate D — the material.** A two-page prototype brief in English
  and Ukrainian, built from the white paper and the proposal and
  saying the stage plainly, that Michel can send to any of them; a
  one-page sketch of the scientific case study for the research
  candidates; and, for each short-listed candidate, three
  sentences in their own frame. Output:
  `docs/partners/PROTOTYPE_BRIEF_EN.md` and `_UK.md`,
  `docs/partners/STUDY_SKETCH_v0_1.md`. The approaches themselves
  are Michel's; the conversation records in the short list what
  was sent and what came back, on his word.

## 5. Open — for Michel, at Gate A or when he wants

- **The grantee.** Until the core body exists, who would receive
  money: Michel personally, a fiscal sponsor, or no money until
  the body is formed — which decides which funders are even
  possible.
- **The ceiling.** Whether the pilot is one grant of the
  recommended size or several small ones, and whether a membership
  scheme on the Via Transilvanica model is wanted at all.
- **The scientific partner's standing.** An academic partner
  studies the prototype; the agreement gives a funder no say over
  publication and gives a partner initiative a corridor — a
  research partner is neither. Which part of the agreement they
  sign, and what they may publish, is for his word, with the
  consent rule of S-14 as the floor.
- **The hromada councils.** Whether they are approached as
  implementers at Gate C or only shown the corridor as S-14
  proposes — the stress tests warn both ways.
- **The country.** Whether Romanian partners (Maramureș, the
  grassland studies, Via Transilvanica itself) are in scope now as
  Phase 3 precursors, or only after the corridor is walked.
- **What Michel will not take.** Money or partnership on terms he
  refuses in advance — a calendar, ratings, a commission, a logo
  on the map, an exclusive, a host's data — so the long list can
  drop them early.

## 6. Working rules

- Every statement about a candidate carries a source and a date;
  what could not be verified is marked and not repeated as fact.
- No approach to anyone, and no email drafted as if from Michel,
  without his word for that candidate.
- Nothing produced here names, pictures or locates a host, and the
  prototype brief says plainly that there is no host yet.
- Documents under the project's standard — front matter,
  changelog, English first, Ukrainian for what will leave the
  project — in `docs/partners/`, committed as Michel Garand from a
  pulled tree; pull before any edit; the starting prompt bumped
  when a gate closes.
- At the end of each working session: what was pushed, what Michel
  should pull, and which decisions in the checklist the next gate
  waits on.
