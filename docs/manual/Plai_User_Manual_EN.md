# Plai — User Manual

*Entering places on the Carpathian map, and publishing them*

October 2026 · UBEC / Carpathian Hub Initiative

## Who this is for

Plai is the Carpathian map of UBEC Mapservice. Trans-Carpathian Pathways uses it to find the people who keep mountain life alive: hosts who offer a bed, a meal, guidance or a workshop, and the places worth walking to.

This manual is for two kinds of people:

| You are | You use Plai to |
| --- | --- |
| An **entrant** — a host, a pathway steward, anyone with an account | Put your own places on the map and keep them up to date |
| A **curator** | Read what entrants send and decide what the public sees |

Chapters 1 to 6 are for everyone who enters places. Chapter 7 is for curators.

Plai's screens are in English for now. This manual quotes every button and field exactly as it appears on the screen.

## 1. Your account

**Getting an account.** Accounts are made by the administrator, Michel Garand. Write to stewardship@ubec.network with the name you want to log in with and an email address. Every account needs its own email address. You receive a user name and a password.

**Logging in.**

1. Open **mapservice.ubec.network**. The page lists the maps, under the heading "Applications".
2. Open the Plai map from its card: click its title or its picture.
3. In the toolbar at the top right, click the log-in icon, an arrow pointing into a door ("Log in to add places").
4. Enter your "User name" and "Password" and click "Login".
5. You return to the list of maps. Open the Plai map again. A pencil icon now appears in the toolbar: that is your way in.

**Logging out.** Click the log-out icon at the right end of the toolbar, an arrow pointing out of a door.

**Your session lasts one hour.** After an hour without logging in again, Plai asks you to sign in, and anything you had not saved is lost. Save before you step away.

**A forgotten password.** The login page offers "Forgot password?", but the service sends no email yet. Write to stewardship@ubec.network instead, and the administrator will set a new password for you.

## 2. The map page

The toolbar along the top holds, from left to right:

| Icon | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Layer tree | Opens the list of map layers on the left; tick or untick to show or hide them |
| Information (i) | Click a point on the map to read what it carries |
| "Place, peak or river" | Search for a town, village, peak or river and jump to it |
| C | The credits: whose data and map style you are looking at |
| Pencil | Opens "Entries", where you enter and edit your places. Shown only once you are logged in |
| Arrow and door | Log in, or log out |

The zoom buttons are on the right edge of the map. Drag the map to move it; use the mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom.

## 3. Entering a place

1. Click the **pencil**. The "Entries" panel opens on the left, showing "My places": the list of everything you have entered.
2. Zoom in on the map until you can see exactly where the place is. The point is published where you put it, so take care.
3. In the panel, click **"Draw point"**. Then click once on the map where the place is.
4. A form opens, titled "A place on the map". Fill it in (chapter 4).
5. At the bottom of the form, choose the **"State"** (chapter 5) and click **"Save"**.

The place appears in "My places" and stays on the map as a point.

**To change a place**, click it in "My places", or click its point on the map. The form opens again; change what you need and click "Save".

**To move a point**, click "Move" in the panel, drag the point to where it belongs, and save. A point outside the area Plai covers is refused.

**Only points.** Plai records each place as a single point. It offers no lines or areas.

## 4. The form, field by field

Fields marked with a star must be filled in; the form will not save without them.

### What the place is

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| "Name of the place" \* | The name people know it by: "Ivan's farm", "The spring above Kryvorivnia" |
| "Type of place" \* | What kind of place it is: a farm, a guesthouse, a shepherd's hut, a spring, a viewpoint, and so on |
| "Category — what this place mainly is" \* | What the place is mainly for: food and produce, lodging, crafts, guiding and walks, and so on. Choose one |
| "Offering" | In your own words, what you offer or what is here. Keep it honest and specific |

The type says what the place is; the category says what it is mainly for. A farm (type) may be mainly about food and produce (category) and still offer a bed to travelers, which goes below.

### If you host travelers

Leave this part empty if you do not host anyone.

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| "What you offer" | Any of: Overnight stay, A meal, Local guidance, A workshop |
| "Beds" | How many people you can put up for the night |
| "People at a meal", "People on a walk", "People at a workshop" | How many people you can take at once, for each offer |
| "… — how you are paid" | For each offer, the forms you accept: Fixed contribution, Suggested contribution, Exchange of work, ubec-pay or a community currency. Optional: leave it empty and the Curator will give your terms |
| "Months you are open" | The months you can receive people. Leave it empty if you would rather travelers ask the Curator |
| "Languages you speak" | Every language you can welcome people in |
| "How to reach this place" | On foot only, By car, With a pram, With a wheelchair. "On foot only" and "By car" cannot both be chosen |

Capacities are whole numbers from 1 to 99. A capacity or a form of payment for an offer you did not tick is not kept: tick the offer first.

To choose several items in a list, click in the field and pick them one at a time. To remove one, click the small × beside it.

No amount of money appears on the map. If you charge a fixed or suggested contribution, the Curator holds the figure.

### Photo

"Photo — JPEG, PNG or WebP". Click "Choose a photo" and pick one picture of the place, up to 10 MB. Its location, camera and date data are removed before it is kept, and it is reduced to at most 1,600 pixels. Any other kind of file is discarded.

A photo is public only if you tick "My photo may be shown on the public map" **and** a curator chooses to show it. Think before you send a photo of your house.

### Contact and consent

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| "Contact name" | The name travelers should ask for |
| "Email" \* | An address where the Curator can reach you about this place |
| "Mobile" | A phone number, if you want one shown |
| "I agree that these contact details are stored with this entry" \* | Your agreement that Plai keeps these details. Required |
| "My contact name may be shown on the public map" | Tick only if you agree |
| "My email may be shown on the public map" | Tick only if you agree |
| "My mobile may be shown on the public map" | Tick only if you agree |
| "My photo may be shown on the public map" | Tick only if you agree |

Each tick is your permission, not a promise of publication: a curator still decides. Untick a box at any time and that detail leaves the public map.

## 5. What happens to a place

Every place has a **state**. You set it in the "State" list at the bottom of the form.

| You choose | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Send to the curator for publication" | The place waits for a curator. The public does not see it yet |
| "Keep as a draft — only you see it" | Nobody else sees it, not even the curator. Finish it later |
| "Withdraw — take it off the map" | The place leaves every public map and every export. Nothing is deleted; you can send it again later |

When a curator publishes it, its state becomes **Published** and the public sees it.

**Changing a published place.** If you change anything in a published place, its name, its offers, its point, it goes back to the curator and leaves the public map until it is published again. Changing a consent tick alone does not: unticking one simply takes that detail off the map.

**Leaving Plai.** To have all your places and your account deleted, write to stewardship@ubec.network.

## 6. What the public sees

Anyone who opens the Plai map, without logging in, sees published places only:

- **The point**, exactly where it was placed. A host, a place with at least one offer, is drawn in ochre, the other places in red.
- **Clicking a point** with the information tool shows its name, type, category, offering, offers, months, languages, how to reach it, capacities and forms of payment.
- **The contact name, email, mobile and photo** appear only where the entrant ticked the box **and** the curator chose to show it.

Drafts, places waiting for a curator, and withdrawn places are never public.

## 7. For curators

A curator reads what entrants send and decides what is published. A curator never changes an entrant's answers: the database puts them back if one tries.

### Opening the queue

Log in and click the pencil. If your account can also enter places, a list at the top of the "Entries" panel switches between "My places" and **"Curation"**. Choose "Curation".

The list shows every place that is waiting ("Submitted") or public ("Published"), with the place's name and who entered it ("Entered by"). Drafts and withdrawn places do not appear: they are the entrant's own business.

### Reading a place

Click a place in the list, or its point on the map. The "Curation" form shows everything the entrant gave, read-only. The hosting fields are shown as short codes:

| Field | Codes |
| --- | --- |
| Offers | lodging (overnight stay), meal, guiding, workshop |
| Terms | fixed, suggested, work (exchange of work), ubec (ubec-pay or a community currency) |
| Months open | jan, feb, mar, … dec |
| Languages | uk, en, de, fr, pl, ro, sk, hu, cs, rue (Rusyn), rom (Romani), ru |
| Reach | foot (on foot only), car, pram, wheelchair |

### Publishing

1. Read the place. Is the offer honest and specific? Is the point where the place is?
2. Under "What the public sees", tick what may be shown: "Show the contact name", "Show the email", "Show the mobile", "Show the photo". A field can be shown only where the entrant agreed, which the box above each one says. A "Show" tick without the entrant's agreement is refused when you save.
3. Set "State" to **"Published"** and click "Save".

To take a place off the public map for a while, set its state back to **"Submitted"**. Only the entrant can withdraw a place.

**Before a host's home goes public**, remember that a published point, in wartime, with a name on it, is an address. The point is the entrant's choice; publishing it is yours.

### Entering places yourself

A curator whose account is also an entrant can enter places: for a host who cannot do it themselves, and for places along the way, such as a viewpoint or a spring.

- What you enter is **your** row, under your name. The host cannot edit or withdraw it; you do it for them.
- Keep the host's agreement on paper. The consent ticks in the form are then yours, on their behalf.
- A place nobody hosts still needs a category and an email: choose "Something else", and give your own email.

## 8. When something goes wrong

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| The form will not save; a field is outlined | A required field is empty, or a number is outside 1 to 99 |
| The save is refused with an error | Most often: "On foot only" and "By car" were both chosen, or the point lies outside the area Plai covers. For a curator: a "Show" tick where the entrant did not agree |
| "Your session has expired" | Log in again; anything not saved is lost. If it appears straight after logging in, the action you tried is not allowed for your account: tell the administrator what you did |
| No pencil in the toolbar | You are not logged in, or your account is not yet in an entering group: write to the administrator |
| Your place is not on the public map | It is a draft, it waits for a curator, it was changed after publication, or it was withdrawn |

**Help.** Write to Michel Garand, stewardship@ubec.network.
